Our Services

Complete air conditioning and heating solutions for residential and commercial properties across Canberra.

Residential Installation

Canberra's weather can be unpredictable — from freezing winters to scorching summers. We help you be the master of your own environment with expert installation of all residential air conditioning and heating systems. Our team will advise you on the most suitable units for your home, ensuring comfort 24/7.

What's Included

  • Free on-site consultation and quote
  • System sizing and selection advice (split, ducted, multi-head)
  • Full supply and installation
  • Manufacturer warranty + our lifetime installation guarantee
  • Post-install walkthrough and user training
  • Access to hard-to-reach installation locations

Commercial Installation

No job is too big or too small. We install air conditioning systems into offices, retail shops, warehouses and any commercial property. Keep your team productive and your customers comfortable with an efficient climate control system tailored to your space.

What's Included

  • Site assessment and load calculation
  • Custom system design for your workspace
  • Ducted, cassette, and VRF/VRV systems
  • Minimal disruption to business operations
  • Ongoing commercial maintenance plans available

Repairs & Diagnostics

AC not cooling? Heater blowing cold air? Strange noises or odours? Our technicians can diagnose and repair faults on all makes and models. We carry common parts on our vans for faster turnaround so you're back to comfort as quickly as possible.

Common Repairs

  • Refrigerant leaks and regas
  • Compressor and fan motor replacement
  • Electrical fault finding and repair
  • Thermostat and control board issues
  • Drainage and condensation problems
  • All makes and models — Fujitsu, Daikin, Mitsubishi, and more

Maintenance & Servicing

Regular maintenance keeps your system running at peak efficiency, reduces energy bills, and prevents costly breakdowns. We recommend servicing your air conditioning at least once a year — ideally before summer and winter peaks.

Service Checklist

  • Filter cleaning and replacement
  • Coil cleaning (indoor and outdoor units)
  • Refrigerant level check
  • Electrical connection inspection
  • Drainage system flush
  • Performance test and efficiency report
